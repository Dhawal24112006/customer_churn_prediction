# Methodology and statistical safeguards

## Prediction time and target
The IBM snapshot provides a contemporaneous label `Churn` (`Yes`=1). This proof-of-concept predicts the snapshot label given account attributes. A true prospective deployment needs date-indexed feature snapshots collected *before* churn, a target horizon and out-of-time evaluation. No features derived from `Churn` or `customerID` are used. `TotalCharges` might encode exposure correlated with tenure, and can be unavailable at a real scoring timestamp; omit it if that cannot be guaranteed. Data are example records, not a generalization guarantee.

## Statistical EDA
`python -m churn_prediction.eda` runs two-sided chi-square independence tests of categorical attributes versus churn, and Mann–Whitney U two-sided tests on numeric features. H0: categorical independence or equal numerical rank distributions; H1: dependence or distributions differ. Chi-square needs independent observations and sufficiently large expected counts (reported); Mann–Whitney assumes independent observations and measures distributional/rank differences, not automatically a median shift. Chi-square effect size is Cramér's V; numeric rank-biserial effect is `2U/(n1*n0)-1`. Benjamini–Hochberg FDR controls expected false discovery proportion under its assumptions. Significance is not causation, and a large sample can make small effects significant. A production study should also provide uncertainty intervals (e.g. bootstrap CIs) and sampling diagnostics.

## Leakage safeguards
Stratified random split with seed 42: 60% train, 20% validation, 20% test. Transformers (medians, scales and one-hot category vocabularies) are trained on the training fold inside `Pipeline`. GridSearchCV runs on training only using stratified 5-fold CV and average precision. The dummy prior baseline is trained identically. XGBoost is optional. Best algorithm by validation average precision (tie: smaller validation log loss) is chosen *before* the test is used. A business net-value threshold is optimized over validation labels, with the configured campaign cost, hypothetical effectiveness and budget. Only then is held-out test performance reported. The final artifact intentionally remains fit on training only, so validation remains a genuine evaluation set for comparing and setting policies. Refit to training+validation would require careful frozen preprocessing and an additional independent final test.

## Metrics
Accuracy may obscure minority churn performance. Precision reflects usefulness of contacts, recall reflects captured churners, F1 balances them, average precision summarizes precision/recall ranking, ROC-AUC ranking across FPR, log loss penalizes poor probabilities and reliability plot tests calibration. Expected costs reflect assumptions, not realized returns. Cost-sensitive probability thresholds should be calibrated if used operationally. A positive class (`Churn==Yes`) is mapped to the exact `classes_==1` probability column.

## Features and interpretation
Use tenure as numeric and categorical age bands (0–11, 12–23, 24–47, 48+), number of six optional add-on services, and month-to-month indicator. Retain raw features as controls. Optional add-on count does not imply a causal intervention lever. Avoid ratios such as `TotalCharges/tenure` when the denominator is zero and when this duplicates monthly billing. Logistic coefficients correspond to log odds **holding other encoded inputs fixed**, subject to collinearity and scaling; `exp(beta)` gives a conditional odds ratio per encoded unit, not a causal risk ratio. Logistic model uses standardized numerical variables. The other pipelines do not scale. All categorizations and imputation are inside the pipeline.

## Explainability and ethics
Validation permutation importance quantifies association with average precision; SHAP analyzes model output in probability units under a sampled background and cannot establish why a person churns. Many one-hot variables are correlated. SHAP calculation is limited to a handful of customers for laptop feasibility. Evaluate fairness, calibration and subgroup errors with representative consented data before business use. No treatment response labels are present, thus no uplift model can be validated. Validate interventions with a randomized controlled trial.
